## 8. Code-only behaviors (what the menus enforce or display with no register rule behind it)

1. A ★ marks the 1,000 column as "most popular quantity" on every grid (`config/site.json:8`).
2. An em-dash appears where a break is absent. Dressed cells are all-or-dash: one missing CAP/CAPW break blanks the whole dressed cell (`src/model.py:174`).
3. A SKU with no breaks shows its reference price in the first column (`src/model.py:143`, `:347`). This is the path the STKR-CUSTOM guard exists to block.
4. Size variants with identical curves auto-collapse into one row (CG 2oz black/clear) and re-split automatically if a reprice makes them differ (`src/model.py:202`; check 11).
5. A qualifier shared by every row is hoisted into a section note ("3mm vinyl", "Violet glass; sticker applied").
6. Trio row order is anchored on jar-alone price, with bundle-only sizes last. Jar-alone rows repeat, untiered, in every tier table.
7. "SINGLES FROM $X" is computed as the lowest @1 jar-alone price (`src/model.py:164`, `:308`).
8. Per-tier MEDIA/FINISHES subtitles are a structured restatement of the tier canon, not verbatim (`config/disclaimers.json:34`; check 15).
9. Touch counts are condensed in the legends: mylar rows read "· 1 sticker" / "· 2 stickers"; o2 reads "BOXES : 2–5 STICKERS BY SHAPE" (K19 is cart 2, display 5, master 5).
10. A generic site-wide exclude-prefix guard exists (`config/site.json:22`), beyond the STKR-CUSTOM case H257 names.
11. The DIFF price audit keeps a git-tracked snapshot, including the sticker grid version (`STKR-GRID`) and the 165 chart cells.
12. The forbidden-string scan is case-insensitive and covers HTML only (`src/build.py:88`).
13. The sticker fetch is paced at 2.2 s per call for the endpoint's 30/min limit; the cache is written atomically; mixed grid versions are refused; 17b degrades to a [DATA] gap after a re-survey.
14. Presentation: hero banners, image-led hub tiles, the page-1 PDF band, hash-routed hub tabs, DOWNLOAD PDF in a new tab.
15. Robots and sitemap are generated (H266 covers indexable, not these files).
16. The build-date stamp uses the runner's UTC date (`src/build.py:155`).
17. The terpene liability disclaimer and the "*MAY NOT PASS LABORATORY TESTING" lines (`config/disclaimers.json:4`; check 6e). July approval, not in the register.
18. Infusion shows "Please inquire for pricing on larger orders." alongside the 500+ INQUIRE row (H263 covers only the INQUIRE row).
19. O2 tables use their own columns: custom wrap 50–5,000, devices 50–100,000.

## Doc-vs-doc disagreements found

1. **Flavor count:** R24 and this order say 169; H96, §10, §19 and the config say 246 (169 + the 77 approved Sep 30).
2. **New Miron sizes:** R26 and this order say five; §31a ("6B the six new sizes") and the handbook (Ch 10, p38) list six, including Saturn 100 Tall.
3. **Menus suite count:** §19 says 26; §34d and H265 say 31 (= O54).
4. **Three-way mechanism:** H260 and §19 say `pre_families`/`post_families`; the code uses `consolidated_ways`.
5. **Tier wording:** the Owner's verbatim canon says "colour" (the menus); handbook Ch 9 says "color". Separately, H264 says "mylar untiered", but R22/K27 say *direct-print* mylar is untiered (sticker-applied mylar is tiered on the menus).
6. **Skip-application framing:** R27 ("deduction = −$0.35") vs K18/H46/H49 (a separate application line; self-apply = no fee). The menus follow K18/H46.
7. **Cap-sticker fee:** R26 says cap stickers carry no separate application fee; the Oct 12 touch table (K19, H47, handbook p38) makes the cap top and cap wrap each a $0.35 touch. The menus say "UP TO 3 STICKERS".
8. **Flavor source:** R24/spec 5.4 (curated config) vs handbook Ch 20, R13 and the forms README (base flavors appear on the public menu).
9. **On Form:** this order says On Form=false items never render; R23/K30/H59/H63 make JAR-MIRON, JAR-CG, CAP and CAPW Off Form yet shown.
10. **Seal:** this order expects the Ozark seal "as an add-on" on the menus; the Oct 12 ruling and H65/H259 keep it off the page.
11. **Media gates:** H218/§13 (every AI asset: no tubes/vapes/droppers) vs §33d (Set 1 menu heroes approved) and this order's menus carve-out.
12. **Nightly time:** H254/§19 say 08:00 UTC; observed starts are 11:48–15:05 UTC.
13. **Archive effect:** the Oct 20 note expected a "33-removal log line"; archiving left the records in place, so no removal lines will appear.
14. **One-stop-shop:** H212 vs the July 17 signed direct-to-bag copy "IN-HOUSE".
15. **Supplier guard:** the base's 10 Suppliers vs the menus' forbidden list (4 of the 10 present, plus 2 names that aren't supplier records).
